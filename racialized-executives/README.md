# Racialized Executives Network / Réseau des cadres racialisés

Bilingual public information website for the network, with sourced aggregate data on racialized executives in Canada's federal public service.

## Contents

- Network introduction, proposed activities, GCXchange link and a contribution form. The homepage identifies Tim as executive founder using the name provided by the site owner. Georges Fattouche is no longer named in the hero or main contact text.
- Executive representation by level, 2017–2025, including executive workforce availability benchmarks.
- Racialized subgroup representation and published Black executive shares by level for 2025.
- Executive hires, promotions and separations for 2024–2025.
- PSC promotion study results for three executive career pathways and six overlapping periods.
- 2024 PSES comparisons for racialized and non-racialized executives, with 207 questions in the reviewed collection. Organizational coverage includes the public service aggregate and 68 organizational results, including micro-organizations combined.
- Historical 2022 PSES executive results, with the original comparator clearly identified.
- CHRC audit findings and source links.

## Files and use

`index.html` is self-contained. Its data tables, language switching and filtered CSV exports work when the file is opened locally. The links to complete CSV and JSON downloads need the accompanying files in the same directory, or the live website.

English can be selected with `?lang=en`; French with `?lang=fr`. The language preference is stored locally in the visitor's browser. The page uses no analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, external scripts or data submission forms.

The GCXchange destination is the clean URL supplied for the network. It may require a Government of Canada work account and is described as under development. Email links open the visitor's email application. The website does not send email.

## Data interpretation

TBS executive employment equity data cover the core public administration and include Law Management (LC). They do not cover every federal employer, deputy ministers or Governor in Council appointees. Executive workforce availability applies to the executive population as a whole; it is not a benchmark for each individual EX level.

Representation depends on voluntary self-identification. Survey results depend on participation and eligibility. Suppressed and unavailable values are retained and are never reconstructed or treated as zero. PSES response counts are rounded, and combined percentages can exclude don't-know or not-applicable responses. Conditional questions apply only to the population described beside the question.

PSC promotion estimates are relative differences from a statistical model, not individual promotion probabilities or annual percentages promoted. Significance markers are retained. The six study periods overlap. Executive staffing movement shares are separate measures with different denominators.

The reviewed 2023 and 2025 Staffing and Non-Partisanship Survey data do not provide a verified racialized-by-executive cross-tab for this page. Broader employee and manager results are not relabelled as racialized executive findings.

`sources.json` records official references and the commits of all 10 reviewed repositories. The underlying Drive archive is identified by title, modification date and checksum. No private Drive document or personal employee record is published.

Source review: 6 November 2026. This date is not the reference period of every series. Data are a fixed snapshot and do not refresh automatically. Check original publications and their terms before further reuse.

## Accessibility checks

Semantic headings and navigation, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, named controls, table captions and headers, text equivalents for charts, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts. English and French rendering, keyboard entry, filters, a generated CSV download, and layouts at 320 and 390 pixels were checked. This is not an independent accessibility certification.

## Français

Site public bilingue du Réseau des cadres racialisés. Il comprend la présentation du réseau, les coordonnées professionnelles fournies, les données de représentation et de progression de carrière, les résultats du SAFF et des liens vers les sources officielles.

Le fichier `index.html` est autonome pour les tableaux, le changement de langue et l'exportation des vues filtrées. Les téléchargements complets de fichiers CSV et JSON nécessitent les fichiers connexes dans le même dossier ou l'accès au site publié. Choisir `?lang=fr` pour ouvrir la version française.

Les populations, périodes et dénominateurs diffèrent selon la source. Les valeurs supprimées restent indisponibles. Un écart entre groupes n'en établit pas les causes. Les résultats du SAFF de 2022 utilisent un groupe de comparaison différent de celui de la vue de 2024. Les limites et les sources sont précisées sur la page.

Les données constituent un portrait daté. Elles ne se mettent pas à jour automatiquement. La page est hébergée sur le site GitHub du RAAR et n'est pas un site ministériel du gouvernement du Canada.

## Contribution form

The English and French form provides twelve contribution categories, optional ideas and experience, availability, preferred language, and a proposed first step. Name, email, a contribution category or idea, and permission to be contacted are required. Native checkboxes support selecting multiple categories. Labels, fieldsets, legends, inline errors, linked error summaries and focus management support keyboard and assistive technology use. Changing language retains entered values.

Responses are processed only in the visitor's browser and are not saved to website storage. The review step shows the full response and exact recipient, georges.fattouche@ssc-spc.gc.ca, the existing contact from the network launch notice. The Send option opens a prefilled email; the visitor must select Send in their email application. This is not an automatic delivery service. Copy and plain-text download options support visitors without a configured email handler or where a long mailto message is not transferred completely. A blank text template is available. No third-party form service, database or email credentials are used.

The founder's surname and email were not supplied or verified. No surname or new recipient address has been inferred. The existing network contact is retained for contribution routing.
