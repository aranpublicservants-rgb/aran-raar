# Racialized Executives Network / Réseau des cadres racialisés

Bilingual network information, contribution opportunities, leadership resources and published evidence about racialized executives in Canada’s federal public service.

## Data coverage

- Executive representation by level, 2017–2025, including EX and LC.
- Racialized subgroup representation and published Black executive results for 2025.
- Executive hires, promotions and separations in 2024–2025.
- PSC promotion models for three career pathways and six overlapping periods.
- 2024 PSES results with complete response measures; executive comparisons for the public service aggregate and 68 organizational result sets, including combined micro-organizations.
- Eleven selected survey questions in a separate organization comparison table.
- Historical 2022 PSES executive results with their original comparator.
- CHRC employer-level findings on barriers to management and executive careers.
- Executive and overall workforce representation trends, 2017–2025.

Data reference periods vary. Source links and interpretation notes accompany the tables. Suppressed and unavailable results are not reconstructed. Survey differences are descriptive and have not been tested for statistical significance. Employer audit counts are separate from employee survey percentages. Workforce shares are separate from workforce availability benchmarks.

## Files

`index.html` contains the interactive page and its data. Companion CSV and JSON files provide downloads. `data-catalog.json` lists the files and reference periods. `network-data.zip` contains the page, data tables, source documentation and this README for offline use.

Select English with `?lang=en` or French with `?lang=fr`. The contribution form retains entered answers when the language changes. Personal form responses are not stored by the website. Visitors review their response, then use their email application to send the prepared message to the network contact. Copy and plain-text download options are available.

The page uses native checkboxes, programmatic form labels, fieldsets and legends, linked errors, keyboard focus indicators, text equivalents for charts and scrollable data tables. Menu controls and form layouts adapt to small screens. External providers control access to their resources and programs.

This employee network resource is hosted on GitHub Pages and is not a Government of Canada departmental website.

## Français

Ressource bilingue du Réseau des cadres racialisés. Elle comprend les possibilités de contribution, les ressources de leadership et les données publiées sur la représentation, la progression de carrière et l’expérience au travail.

Les périodes, les populations et les groupes de comparaison varient selon les séries. Les valeurs supprimées et indisponibles ne sont pas reconstituées. Les écarts descriptifs ne suffisent pas à en établir les causes. Le formulaire ouvre un courriel préparé; la personne doit sélectionner Envoyer dans sa messagerie.
