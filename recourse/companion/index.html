PEOPLE HELP PEOPLE

Connect with someone who has been there

Choose someone you trust, such as a colleague with recourse experience, an employee network you already belong to, or a union representative. You decide whom to contact. This website does not collect, match, forward, or report your experience to ARAN. Peers can share what helped them, not provide legal advice.

The buttons copy generic text to your clipboard; they do not email ARAN or anyone else. You choose the recipient and method. Do not include confidential case details when first reaching out. This is not a formal complaint.

OPTIONAL PRIVATE AI

Explain it with on-device AI

The assistant's verified pathways and case links work without AI. On supported devices, a small language model can help explain the selected information in plain language. The model runs on your device, not a paid cloud API.

Optional. Requires a compatible graphics-enabled browser and a substantial one-time model download. It may not work on every phone or workplace device.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

What this cannot do

  • It cannot decide whether discrimination legally occurred.
  • It cannot file grievances, preserve deadlines or contact your employer.
  • It does not replace a union representative, lawyer, human-rights commission or qualified support person.
  • A peer connection is not a confidential ombuds or legal service.
