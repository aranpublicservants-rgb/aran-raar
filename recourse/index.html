Time limits matter

Informal discussions, mediation, ombuds services, requests for records and requests for reconsideration do not automatically stop legal filing deadlines. Verify the specific rule promptly.

Three things to check first

The legal route depends on the employer, job and issue. The tools below are a starting point, not a legal eligibility ruling.

  1. 1Confirm your employer

    Core federal administration, separate agencies, Crown corporations and military or police roles do not all follow the same rules.

  2. 2Confirm your employment status

    A term employee may still be union-represented. An excluded position, casual appointment, student role or executive position needs separate review.

  3. 3Check the deadline now

    The first relevant notice or when you learned of a decision may start the clock. Do not assume you have 25 working days.

Why there is no automatic deadline calculator

The start date, counting rules, holidays, extensions, agreement provisions and legal jurisdiction can change the result. An incorrect date could cause a missed filing. Use the official filing instructions and obtain advice.
