1. Purpose and scope

This working document gathers practical lessons about implementing the Clerk’s Call to Action on Anti-Racism, Equity, and Inclusion. It provides a structure for a future synthesis paper.

2. Method and limits

Contributions are voluntary and are not a representative sample. Reported experiences, published evidence and recommendations must be distinguished. Check sources, dates, duplicate examples and missing perspectives before drawing conclusions. Contributions remain in their original language.

3. Contributions by theme

Shared contributions will appear here after the connection is checked.

4. Cross-cutting analysis

Compare effective actions, persistent barriers, necessary resources and conditions for change. Distinguish individual examples from patterns supported by several sources. Record evidence gaps and counterexamples.

5. Recommendations to develop

For each recommendation, specify the barrier, proposed action, responsible role, timeline, resources, measure of progress and review date. Contributions are not government or network commitments.

6. Review before publication

Verify evidence and consent, remove identifying information, seek review from people with relevant lived experience, and review both languages and accessibility.
