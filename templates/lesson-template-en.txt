ARAN | Lesson learned contribution

Describe a general lesson about putting anti-racism into practice. A useful lesson can come from an approach that worked, failed or produced mixed results.

Before writing
Remove names, email addresses, personal case details and protected information.
Describe roles and processes without identifying individuals or organizations.
Use public references only. Do not attach internal records or private correspondence.

Theme
Choose the closest theme: leadership and accountability; hiring and career development; data and measurement; employee networks and participation; learning and workplace culture; accessibility and intersecting barriers; language and regional access; or another practical improvement.

5. Lesson learned
